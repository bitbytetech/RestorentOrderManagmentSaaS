How RestroHub QR Ordering Works

The proven Scan → Order → Kitchen workflow built for maximum speed and customer comfort.

1
Scan the QR Code

Guests scan the table QR code (e.g. /o/hazratganj/t/4). No app installation or sign-up required.

2
Browse & Order

Guests choose portion/weight variants (Full/Half/250g), add special cooking notes, and place the order.

3
Kitchen Gets It Instantly

Orders appear on the cashier POS screen and kitchen display (KDS) immediately for fast preparation.
